# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** August 25, 2026

## Overview

Pinpal ("we", "us", "our", or "the App") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our application.

## Information We Collect

### Location Data
- **Geolocation Information**: When you use the "Save Current Location" feature, we collect your device's geographic coordinates (latitude and longitude). This data is stored locally on your device.
- **Local Storage Only**: Location data is stored exclusively in your browser's local storage and is never transmitted to our servers.

### Usage Analytics
- **Google Analytics**: We use Google Analytics (GA4) to understand how users interact with our application. This helps us improve functionality and user experience.
- **Anonymized Data**: Analytics data is anonymized and does not identify you personally.

### Device Information
- **Browser and Device Details**: We may collect information about your device type, operating system, and browser to optimize app performance.

## How We Use Your Information

1. **Core Functionality**: Your location data is used solely to enable the core features of Pinpal (saving, displaying, and navigating to saved locations).
2. **Service Improvement**: Analytics data helps us improve the app's performance, features, and user experience.
3. **Technical Support**: We may use non-personal technical data to troubleshoot issues and ensure service reliability.

## Data Storage and Security

- **Local Storage**: All saved locations are stored locally on your device. We do not have access to this data.
- **No Cloud Backup**: Currently, Pinpal does not backup location data to cloud servers.
- **Encryption**: We recommend using a secure device and keeping your browser updated for optimal security.

## Third-Party Services

Our app integrates with:

- **Google Maps API**: For displaying maps and navigation features. Google's privacy practices apply to data shared with Google Maps.
- **Google Analytics**: For usage analytics. See [Google Analytics Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
- **Social Sharing**: When you share via WhatsApp, Twitter, Facebook, or native share, those platforms' privacy policies apply.

## Your Privacy Rights

- **Access Your Data**: You can view all saved locations directly in the app.
- **Delete Your Data**: You can delete individual locations or clear all data using the app's interface. Clearing data deletes your local storage.
- **No Personal Account**: Pinpal does not require account creation, so no personal identifying information is collected or stored by us.

## Data Retention

- **Location Data**: Retained locally on your device until you delete it.
- **Analytics Data**: Retained by Google Analytics according to their policies (typically 14-26 months).

## Children's Privacy

Pinpal is not intended for use by children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we become aware that a child has provided us with personal information, we will take steps to delete such information.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last Updated" date will reflect any changes. Your continued use of the app following the posting of revised Privacy Policy means you accept and agree to the changes.
