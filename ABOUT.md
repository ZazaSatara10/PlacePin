# About Pinpal

## What is Pinpal?

Pinpal is a lightweight, privacy-first web application that helps you save and navigate to your favorite locations using Google Maps. Whether you're exploring a new city or need to remember where you parked, Pinpal makes it easy to pin important places and find your way back.

## Key Features

✨ **Save Locations** - Capture your current location with an optional custom name.

🗺️ **Quick Navigation** - Open saved locations in Google Maps with one click.

📍 **Travel Modes** - Choose between walking, driving, bicycling, or transit navigation.

💾 **Local Storage** - All your locations are stored securely on your device—no account needed.

📱 **Progressive Web App** - Install Pinpal as an app on your phone or access it from any browser.

🔒 **Privacy First** - Your location data stays on your device. We never upload or track your locations.

🌐 **Offline Capable** - Works seamlessly with or without an internet connection (service worker enabled).

📲 **Easy Sharing** - Share locations via WhatsApp, Twitter, Facebook, or copy links directly.

## Technology Stack

- **HTML5** - Semantic markup and modern web standards
- **JavaScript** - Dynamic interactions and geolocation APIs
- **Service Worker** - Offline support and app-like experience
- **Google Maps API** - Map display and navigation
- **Local Storage** - Device-local data persistence
- **PWA (Progressive Web App)** - Install-to-homescreen capability

## How It Works

1. **Allow Location Access**: Grant the app permission to access your device's location.
2. **Save a Location**: Click "Save Current Location" and optionally name it.
3. **View Your Pins**: All saved locations appear in the "Saved Locations" section.
4. **Navigate**: Click "Navigate" to open directions in Google Maps.
5. **Share**: Share locations with friends via your preferred method.
6. **Manage**: Edit, delete, or clear all locations as needed.

## Why Pinpal?

- **No Sign-Up Required**: Start using immediately without creating an account.
- **Lightweight**: Fast load times and minimal resource usage.
- **Reliable**: Built with progressive enhancement for consistent experience.
- **Accessible**: Fully keyboard navigable and screen reader friendly.
